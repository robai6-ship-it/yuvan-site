Yuvan's reading app

Help every autistic child learn to read, free.

Yuvan is autistic. Reading didn't click for him the usual way. Then we tried a program that teaches reading through singing, and his reading took off.

Songs gave him a reason to look at the words and the patience to see them again and again. Not every family can pay for a program like that, or find one at all. We want to build a sing-along reading app and give it free to every family with an autistic child.

What we'll build

Words light up as they're sung

Children hear, see and follow each word many times, without it feeling like a drill.

Calm by design

No flashing, no countdown timers, no surprise sounds. Volume and speed are easy to change.

The same routine every time

Each lesson follows a predictable pattern with a picture schedule, so children know what comes next.

Works for non-speaking children

Answers are a tap, not a spoken word, so every child can take part.

Small steps, visible progress

Short songs, gentle checks and a simple progress view for parents and therapists.

Free and private

No ads, no fees, no selling data. Children's privacy is protected under US law (COPPA), and we'll go beyond it.

Questions

Is this the same as TUNE into READING?

No. Yuvan used TUNE into READING, and it helped him. Its makers now run the program through a nonprofit, Sing Out and READ. Our app is a new, separate project inspired by what singing did for Yuvan. We're not connected to Electronic Learning Products or Sing Out and READ, and we won't copy their songs or software.

Does singing really help children read?

Many children, autistic or not, find words easier to remember with a tune and a beat, and singing gives a natural reason to repeat them. Research on sing-along reading is encouraging but still small, and very little of it is about autistic children. That's why we'll work with reading specialists and test with families instead of making big promises.

I want to help a child read today. What can I do?

Sing Out and READ is a registered nonprofit that gives its singing-based reading program free to qualifying families with children aged 6 to 12. Gifts to them are tax-deductible.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Not yet. We're not a registered charity today. Once we have nonprofit status from the IRS, we'll say so here and send receipts.

Can I help without giving money?

Yes. We're looking for songwriters and singers, illustrators, reading specialists, speech therapists, autistic adults who'd advise us, and families willing to test early versions. Email us.
