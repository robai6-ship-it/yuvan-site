Songbird Reader

A free sing-along reading app for autistic children.

Yuvan is autistic. Reading didn't click for him the usual way. Then we tried a program that teaches reading through singing, and his reading took off.

Songs gave him a reason to look at the words and the patience to see them again and again. Not every family can pay for a program like that, or find one at all. So we're building our own sing-along reading app, to give away free to every family with an autistic child.

Where we are now: building the first version, with two songs where each word lights up as it's sung. We're making it at home with help from AI tools, on a small budget, and testing it with Yuvan first. The app is called Songbird Reader. It will have its own website soon, and we'll link it here.

What the first version will do

Words light up as they're sung

Children hear, see and follow each word many times, without it feeling like a drill.

Calm by design

No flashing, no countdown timers, no surprise sounds. Volume and speed are easy to change.

Tap, don't talk

Answers are a tap, not a spoken word, so non-speaking children can take part too.

Free and private

No ads, no fees, no accounts for children, and no selling data.

The plan

  1. Now

    Two songs and a simple app that works in a phone or tablet browser. Yuvan tries it first.

  2. Next

    A few families of autistic children test it. We fix what doesn't work.

  3. Then

    More songs and levels, added as we learn what helps.

  4. Later

    Free for every family, on its own Songbird Reader website.

Our promises

  • The app will be free for every family, for good.
  • No ads, and we will never sell or share a child's data.
  • We'll list what we receive and what we spend, here on this page.
  • We'll test with families before calling anything ready.
  • If the project stops, we'll say so plainly and tell supporters where the money went.

Questions

Is this the same as TUNE into READING?

No. Yuvan used TUNE into READING, and it helped him. Its makers now run the program through a nonprofit, Sing Out and READ. Our app is a new, separate project inspired by what singing did for Yuvan. We're not connected to Electronic Learning Products or Sing Out and READ, and we won't copy their songs or software.

Does singing really help children read?

Many children, autistic or not, find words easier to remember with a tune and a beat, and singing gives a natural reason to repeat them. Research on sing-along reading is encouraging but still small, and very little of it is about autistic children. That's why we're starting small and testing with families instead of making big promises.

I want to help a child read today. What can I do?

Sing Out and READ is a registered nonprofit that gives its singing-based reading program free to qualifying families with children aged 6 to 12. Gifts to them are tax-deductible.

Are gifts tax-deductible?

No. This is a family project, not a registered charity. If that changes, we'll say so here.

Can I help without giving money?

Yes. Tell other families about it, or volunteer to test early versions with your child. Songwriters, singers, illustrators, reading specialists, speech therapists and autistic adults who'd share advice are very welcome too. Email us.
